About TeaTime

TeaTime is an independently owned café based in Vancouver, dedicated to bringing high-quality tea sourced from different regions around t he world to our community. We offer a carefully curated selection of loose-leaf teas, allowing customers to explore a variety of flavours, aromas, and tea traditions. Whether you're looking for a refreshing green tea, a comforting black tea, or a soothing herbal blend, we hope to have something for everyone.
