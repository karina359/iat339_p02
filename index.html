About TeaTime

TeaTime is an independently owned café based in Vancouver, dedicated to bringing high-quality tea sourced from different regions around the world to our community. We offer a carefully curated selection of loose-leaf teas, allowing customers to explore a variety of flavours, aromas, and tea traditions. Whether you're looking for a refreshing green tea, a comforting black tea, or a soothing herbal blend, we hope to have something for everyone. Alongside our selection of tea leaves, we also offer a small menu of freshly prepared drinks and light snacks, including lattes, specialty teas, cakes, and other sweet treats. Our goal is to create a cozy and welcoming environment where customers can slow down, relax, study, or catch up with friends while enjoying a comforting drink. Visit our Menu Page to explore our selection of drinks and cakes that we have to offer.
