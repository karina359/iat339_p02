What does TeaTime do?

TeaTime is a modern style cafe based in Vancouver that sells a variety of items ranging from coffee, teas, and cakes. The cafe provides a casual and comfortable environment for all customers to comfortably enjoy our drinks and cakes. With our range of products, we cater to a wide range of customers with different tastes. Students enjoy studying at our cafe given the calm and relaxing space to study and get work done.

Who is the company to potential customers?

Students who want a cozy cafe and a drink to pass the time studying.

Older generation who would want to reminisce on old flavours with a modern twist.

Why purchase from TeaTime?

TeaTime offers a variety of items to cater towards a wider range of people. Our cafe provides a modern, calm, and relaxing environment that people can enjoy, hang out with friends, or get work done.
